What We Are Building

This is the system we have built and are actively advancing. A complete, end-to-end vision drone ecosystem optimized for constrained environments.

Nano-UAV Platform

Developing a vision-guided autonomous nano-UAV specifically for indoor navigation without GPS.

Real-Time Vision

Integrating onboard camera with a lightweight custom CNN for rapid, real-time decision making.

Extreme Edge AI

Running inference at high speed (≈500 FPS peak) with a remarkably small model footprint (≈25 KB).
